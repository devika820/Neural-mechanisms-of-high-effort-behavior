## Research Question: Why do some people keep choosing the hard thing?

Exercise is a classic **high-effort, low-immediate-reward behavior**. Some people stick with it; others don't. **Why?**

We want to understand whether differences in the neural mechanisms underlying **effort, motivation, fatigue, reward, and persistence** can help explain individual differences in exercise engagement.

The clinical setting is a pilot randomized exercise trial among **Veterans with non-dialysis, advanced chronic kidney disease (CKD)**.

We're looking for neuroscience-minded collaborators to help turn this clinical question into a **testable neuroimaging research question**.
